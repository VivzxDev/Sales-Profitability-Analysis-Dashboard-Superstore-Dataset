Superstore Dataset

💡 “Why is the company losing money despite strong sales?”

🔍 What I did:

Analyzed 9,994 transactions across:
🌍 4 Regions (Central, East, South, West)
🗂️ Multiple product categories
Evaluated overall sales vs profitability performance.

📉 Key Insights:

Discovered negative profit margins in several categories.
Pinpointed the root cause:
👉 Excessive and inconsistent discounting strategies
Showed how discounts directly eroded margins despite strong revenues.

📊 Dashboard Highlights:

Interactive Power BI dashboard with:
Region & Category Slicers 🎛️
Profit/Loss Drilldowns 🔍
Dynamic visual storytelling

💡 Business Recommendations:

Optimize discount thresholds 🚫
Implement smarter pricing strategies 💲
Align discounts with profitability goals

🛠️ Tools:
Excel | Power BI
